Presentation

  gameplay portrait, Spine celebration rig and native full-body KingDrill rig.

  and weighted grid bound to the recovered crown/head influences. The original

Rig and activity
- Eye, lid, lash and brow scaling stays coupled; enlarged chestnut irises remain
  inside the painted eye silhouettes. Teeth stabilization remains active.
- Visible input locks and focus/pause transitions retire attention smoothly.
  Fresh gestures at reaction completion are accepted; held gestures do not replay.
- Low-move worry survives shuffle and pause. Committed relief/failure reactions
  retain priority. Pointer, body lean, tap and real board-hint attention verified.
- Disappearing hints and expired pointers discard outward spring velocity before
  settling, preventing the one-frame movement farther toward an obsolete target.
- Skin replacement updates both lookup and enumeration and is idempotent.
