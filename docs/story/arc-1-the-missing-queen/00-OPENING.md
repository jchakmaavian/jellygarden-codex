# OPENING SEQUENCE — First 3 Minutes

Everything before Area 001's second level. This is the only part of the game most players will ever see, so it carries the whole pitch: *a king, a ruined home, a missing wife, and a board you already know how to play.*

---

## RULE ZERO — GAMEPLAY BEFORE STORY

No logo hold. No menu. No cinematic the player has to sit through.

**App opens → a match-3 board is already on screen → first tap is a match.**

The story arrives *between* moves and *after* the win, never before the first one. If a player uninstalls at 0:30, they should still have made twelve matches.

---

## THE HOOK QUESTION

The player must leave this sequence holding exactly one question: **where is the Queen?**

Not "who is Grimm" — he does not appear until Area 006. Not "why is the castle broken" — that reads as a chore list. The hook is a person, missing, and a husband who does not believe the answer he is about to be given.

Leo says her name once, at 1:25, into an empty courtyard. That single unanswered word is the whole game's engine. Do not add a second line. Do not have anyone explain who she is.

---

## WHAT THE OPENING MUST NOT DO

- **No Grimm.** He is Area 006. Showing the villain at minute one turns a mystery into a chore.
- **No task list dump.** Four tasks visible, not forty. The castle's full scale reveals over Areas 002–005.
- **No tutorial for the meta layer.** One task, one star, one visible change. The player infers the rest.
- **No hard fail.** Level 1 cannot be lost — see Area 001 tuning.
- **No paywall, no ad, no offer** inside the first session. Nothing for sale until Area 003.

---

## VOICE — ALFRED

Alfred is a **voice-over only** for the whole of Area 001. He is not standing there. Leo arrives to an empty castle, and the only company is a narrator and a cat — that emptiness is the point.

He appears in person at **Area 005 (Entrance Hall)**, which pays off eleven weeks of silence.

Tone: dry, fond, a servant who has known Leo since he was small. Past tense, storybook. He knows how this ends and is not going to say.

Sample lines for the opening:
- *"The King had been gone eleven weeks."*
- *"He had left the kingdom in good hands. So he was told."*
- *"He expected trumpets. He got weeds."*
- *"No one had thought to tell him. That, in itself, was the answer."*

---

## FIRST SESSION TARGET

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Time to first match | **< 3 seconds** |
| Time to first win | ~60 seconds |
| Time to first repair | ~115 seconds |
| Levels in session 1 | 4–6 (all of Area 001, into 002) |
| Tutorial screens shown | **0** |
| Levels lost in session 1 | **0** |
| Monetisation shown | **none** |

If a player finishes session 1 without seeing an offer, they come back. Area 001 is not a revenue area. It is a promise.
