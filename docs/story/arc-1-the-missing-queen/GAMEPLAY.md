# ARC 1 — MATCH-3 SPEC

How *The Missing Queen* becomes a playable board.

---

## THE LOOP

```
Play level  →  win  →  +1 Star
Spend Star  →  complete 1 repair task
Last task   →  CLUE lands  →  next area unlocks
```

The Star is the only currency between the match-3 layer and the story layer. Keep it that way. One level = one star = one visible change in the world.

---

## CORE PRINCIPLE — THE BOARD IS THE ROOM

Obstacles are never generic. Every blocker is the room's actual problem. The gates are choked with ivy, so the board has vines. The hearth is full of ash, so the board has ash. The player learns the room by playing it — no tutorial copy needed.

---

## SECOND PRINCIPLE — GRIMM AUTHORS THE DIFFICULTY

| Areas | Obstacle behaviour | In-fiction reason |
|---|---|---|
| 001–018 | Static or slow. Rot, dust, rubble, thorns. | Neglect. Nobody has cared for this place. |
| **019** | **Rising water.** Active, timed, hostile. | **Grimm opens the sluice gates.** |
| 019–025 | Obstacles act on their own. **Grimm's Meddling** tile re-locks cleared squares. | Grimm is actively fighting the restoration. |

Area 019 is the hinge of the whole arc. Every hard level after it reads as Grimm fighting back rather than the designer being cruel. Player frustration lands on the villain, which is exactly where you want it.

---

## LEVEL TYPES — ONE DEBUT PER FIVE AREAS

| Type | Debuts | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | 001 | Clear the objective in N moves. |
| **Rescue** | 003 | Escort a character token to the board exit. Recovers an ally. |
| **Evidence** | 009 | Uncover N buried tiles to reveal a document or object. |
| **Race** | 019 | Limited moves against a rising, spreading, or collapsing threat. |
| **Boss** | 025 | Multi-phase. Every obstacle in the arc, in order, then a clean HP bar. |

By Area 025 the player has the full vocabulary. Arc 2 can start combining them without teaching anything new.

## THE CLUE MOMENT

Never fire a clue on a level win. Fire it on the **final repair task**:

```
Last star spent  →  repair animation  →  camera finds the object  →  CLUE card
```

Area 009: the hearth rebuilds, ash falls away, camera pushes in — **Freya's ring**. Freeze frame. That is the screenshot people share.

- **Beat areas (006, 009, 013, 016, 019, 021, 023) and the payoff (025)** — full cutscene.
- **The other 17** — a three-second clue card.

Cheap to produce, and the beats hit harder for the contrast.

---

## THE FREYA PROBLEM

Freya is absent for 24 areas. The risk is obvious: players will not care about rescuing a stranger. She has to become a person through the boards, not through other people talking about her.

| Area | How she speaks |
|---|---|
| 003 | Her mare Juniper is the first Rescue token the player ever escorts. |
| 007 | Her cloak, still hanging. |
| 009 | Her ring, buried in ash — the first Evidence level. |
| 012 | A torn piece of her dress. |
| **014** | **Her seed journal — her own handwriting, read directly.** |
| 021 | Her note under the floorboard: **BELOW.** |
| 022 | Every dress still on the rail. |
| 025 | Freya herself, as a Rescue token. |

The escort verb is taught in Area 003 with a horse and paid off in Area 025 with the queen. That symmetry is the emotional spine of the arc — protect it.
