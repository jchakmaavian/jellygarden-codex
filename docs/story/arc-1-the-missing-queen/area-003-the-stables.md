## Board

**New obstacle:** **Hay bales** — block movement, cleared only by a match made directly on them.

**Level types:** Standard, **Rescue (debut)**

**Tuning note:** Rescue level introduces the character token: bring Juniper down the board to the exit. First time the player escorts a *someone* instead of clearing a *something*. Do this before Freya matters — it teaches the verb early.
