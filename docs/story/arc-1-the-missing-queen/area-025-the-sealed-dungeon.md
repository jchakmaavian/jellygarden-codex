## Board

**New obstacle:** **Cell door** — three-phase boss. Phase 1 rising water, phase 2 Grimm's Meddling at double rate, phase 3 the door's HP bar with no blockers at all. Every obstacle in the arc appears once, in order, then clears away.

**Level types:** **Boss (debut)**, Rescue

**Tuning note:** Final Rescue level is Freya's token — the escort verb taught in Area 3 with a horse, paid off in Area 25 with the queen. Longest area in the arc; it should feel like a climb.
