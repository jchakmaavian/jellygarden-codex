# ARC 1 — The Missing Queen

**Areas:** 001–025
**Question:** Where is Queen Freya?
**Payoff:** Freya found alive in the sealed dungeon. Grimm flees the castle.
**Villain in this arc:** Grimm, the Royal Advisor — still wearing the mask of a loyal servant.
**Setting:** The main castle: gates, halls, kitchens, gardens, library, private chambers.

## Summary

Leo returns from the royal hunt to a rotted castle and a missing wife. Grimm says she left. Every room Leo rebuilds says otherwise. The castle was not neglected — it was collapsed on purpose, to bury the way down to her cell.
