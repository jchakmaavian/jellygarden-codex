## Board

**New obstacle:** **Rubble blocks** — two hits to clear, do not spread. The honest, static counterweight to vines.

**Level types:** Standard

**Tuning note:** Vines + rubble on the same board from level 3. First taste of combining an active obstacle with a passive one.

## Clue

First hint the damage is deliberate. Player should feel it, not be told it.
