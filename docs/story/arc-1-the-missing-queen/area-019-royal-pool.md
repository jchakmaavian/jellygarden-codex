## Board

**New obstacle:** **Rising water** — fills one board row every four turns from the bottom. Drowned tiles are unmatched. Plus **Grimm's Meddling**: from this area on, a marked tile appears mid-level and re-locks one square you already cleared.

**Level types:** Standard, **Race (debut)**

**Tuning note:** The hinge of the whole arc. Race level: beat the water to the sluice gate. Difficulty jumps by design and the story pays for it — every hard level after this reads as Grimm fighting back, not the designer being cruel.

## Clue

> Mid-repair, the sluice gates open on their own. The lower halls flood. This was not neglect — this was done tonight.

## Direction

**ARC BEAT.** Grimm stops pretending. Repairs get harder from here. Justifies the difficulty ramp.
