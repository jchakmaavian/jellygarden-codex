# ARC 2 — Grimm's Trail

**Areas:** 026–050
**Question:** Who helped Grimm? He could not have done this alone.
**Payoff:** The traitor inside the court is named and exposed at the Harvest Court.
**Villain in this arc:** Grimm from the shadows, plus a noble house that fed him keys and signatures.
**Setting:** Beyond the walls: the village, the mill, the docks, the market, the old chapel.

## Summary

Grimm is gone but not alone. Someone opened doors for him. Leo and Freya rebuild the ruined village outside the walls, and every grateful villager remembers a face that shouldn't have been there.
