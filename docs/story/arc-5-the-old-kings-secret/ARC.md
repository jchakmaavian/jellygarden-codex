# ARC 5 — The Old King's Secret

**Areas:** 101–125
**Question:** Why did Grimm hate Leo from the beginning?
**Payoff:** Grimm is Leo's half-brother. The old king hid him. Grimm never forgave either of them.
**Villain in this arc:** Grimm, finally speaking in his own voice.
**Setting:** The buried past: the monastery, the old ruins, the royal crypt, the sunken keep.

## Summary

A sealed crypt, a monk who kept a secret for forty years, and a second name in the old king's records. Leo rebuilds the places his father tried to erase — and finds out the man who buried his wife shares his blood.
