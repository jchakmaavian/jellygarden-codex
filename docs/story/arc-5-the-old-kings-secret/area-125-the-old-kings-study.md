## Clue

> **ARC PAYOFF.** In the hidden drawer: the old king's unsent letter, asking his elder son to come home and take what was his. Leo reads it aloud to Grimm in his cell. Grimm does not believe him — and then does, and breaks. Then he tells them what he has already set in motion, and that it is too late to stop: the army is coming, and it is coming for the castle.

Arc question in play: **Why did Grimm hate Leo from the beginning?**
