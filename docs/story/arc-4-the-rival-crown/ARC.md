# ARC 4 — The Rival Crown

**Areas:** 076–100
**Question:** Who is paying Grimm now?
**Payoff:** A neighbouring crown is revealed as Grimm's patron. War is one insult away.
**Villain in this arc:** Grimm as a warlord's advisor, and the rival monarch behind him.
**Setting:** The border: watchtowers, the frost road, the winter palace, the rival court.

## Summary

Banners appear on the northern ridge. Leo must repair the border forts he let rot and host a peace summit he cannot afford. Freya negotiates. Grimm sits at the other side of the table, smiling, wearing someone else's colours.
