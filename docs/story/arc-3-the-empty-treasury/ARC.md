# ARC 3 — The Empty Treasury

**Areas:** 051–075
**Question:** The kingdom is broke. Where did the gold go?
**Payoff:** The gold bought an army. Grimm is not hiding — he is arming.
**Villain in this arc:** Grimm the fugitive, now a paymaster.
**Setting:** The working kingdom: vaults, the mint, the ore mines, trade roads, the winter granary.

## Summary

The crown cannot pay its own guards. Ledgers are clean, which is the problem — they are too clean. Leo reopens the mines and the mint, and the missing coin leads to a mercenary camp across the river.
