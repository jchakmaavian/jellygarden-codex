## Clue

> **ARC PAYOFF.** Leo and Freya are crowned together, by the kingdom's consent rather than by a document. Aldous stands in the crowd, named, pardoned, and not restored — and that is the ending he accepts. The old king's statue is raised again with both his sons' names on the base.

Arc question in play: **Can the kingdom survive Grimm's last move?**

## Direction

**ARC PAYOFF.** End on the courtyard from Area 002 in full sun. The last repair task lights the whole castle at once. Hold on the wide shot and roll credits.
