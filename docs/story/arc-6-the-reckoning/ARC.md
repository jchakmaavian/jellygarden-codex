# ARC 6 — The Reckoning

**Areas:** 126–150
**Question:** Can the kingdom survive Grimm's last move?
**Payoff:** Grimm defeated. The castle restored to full glory. Leo and Freya crowned together before the whole kingdom.
**Villain in this arc:** Grimm at the head of an army, with nothing left to lose.
**Setting:** The siege and after: outer walls, war camp, the breached castle, and every restored room, rebuilt one final time.

## Summary

Grimm comes back with an army and burns what Leo rebuilt. The last arc is the whole game in reverse — repairing under fire, room by room, until the castle stands and the crown is finally, publicly, undisputed.
