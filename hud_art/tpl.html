The seven sprites behind the level HUD, assembled at the size a phone actually shows them: 393 pt across. Every panel below is a real CSS border-image nine-slice — the same operation Unity's Sliced sprite does — so what stretches here stretches there.

HUD width — drag it

Turn both on and drag the width. Nine-slice holds the gold end caps at a fixed size and tiles only the wood; the naive stretch smears them. That difference is the entire reason the bar ships as slices instead of one picture.
