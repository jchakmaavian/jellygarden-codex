King Vanguard facial rig repair — 4 October 2026

Installed and tested in JellyGarden-Codex using Unity 6000.5.10f1.
The earlier screenshots did not show a readable upper lip. The line identified
as a lip was a teeth/moustache boundary. That assessment was incorrect.

BUG LIST AND REPAIRS

1. Missing readable upper lip and mouth opening.
   Replaced the broad skin-patch mouth with a painted cavity and thin upper and
   lower lips. The original animated mouth geometry drives their position,
   width, opening and corner movement, including native animation crossfades.

2. Teeth drifted, stretched and crossed the lips.
   Upper and lower teeth now share the mouth coordinate system. Their physical
   height is stable and their visible geometry is constrained to the painted
   cavity. Lower teeth disappear when the opening is too small. The first
   regression run caught upper-tooth crossing in Happy_1; contour bounds fixed it.

3. A large exposed jaw/skin patch looked pasted over the beard.
   Removed the old visible mouth patch. A continuous head/beard underlay provides
   the skin beneath the new mouth, including the rounded central chin area.

4. The moustache had disconnected tips, clipped contours and a hard seam.
   Added a connected moustache sprite and a new weighted mesh registered to its
   complete silhouette. It follows the source bones without reusing the old
   incompatible hair deformation mask. Added a small matching contact shadow.

5. Temple gaps, hard cheek patches and beard seams broke facial continuity.
   Replaced the head/beard underlay and ear artwork. Cheek color is part of the
   continuous underlay; the old independent cheek patches are hidden.

6. Crown shadow was detached/diagonal and made the fitted crown look misplaced.
   Retained the calibrated full crown fit and rebuilt its contact shadow from
   the crown's own bottom geometry and weights. Removed obsolete facial shadows.

7. Eyes had rough low-resolution rims/irises and mismatched detail.
   Registered new eye whites with integrated socket edging and independent
   240-pixel iris artwork. Preserved native eyelids/lashes and their blink curves;
   generated lids were rejected during visual QA because they did not register.

8. Upward gaze was nearly blocked during one dance pose.
   The test found the neutral pupil center used the eye bounding box, which can
   lie too close to the curved opening. It now uses the center of the actual
   valid pupil-placement area. Pupil scale remains 1.3 throughout the tests.

9. New artwork was being constrained by old asset silhouettes.
   New parts have their own atlas regions and weighted registration. They are
   not cut by the old masks. Original atlas content and existing GUIDs remain.

10. Runtime lifecycle/visibility could leave an uninitialized or ghost mouth.
    The mouth initializes on activation, hides when its native driver is absent,
    and follows the driver's slot fade. Every actor owns separate mutable
    geometry, so a second King cannot change the first King's expression.

ACTIVITY AND UI VERIFICATION

Existing production activity routing was retained and retested with the new
face. Virtual mouse/touch devices used the production input actions. The main
game's real solver supplied the visible match hint. Tests verified smooth
attention retirement, fresh versus held touch boundaries, pause/focus cleanup,
low-move worry persistence, and committed relief/failure priority. State-only
probes tested priority without granting win rewards or triggering popups.

Main HUD captures show idle and two-thumbs-up celebration in the gold arch.
Canvas tests also cover native UI material, RectMask2D, four expressions and
serialized component activation. Normal player progress was restored after
each live test. No unrelated gameplay or save implementation was changed.

ART AND IMPLEMENTATION

Art/face-parts-clean.png and Art/head-underlay-final.png were made with the
built-in image_gen tool. Exact successful prompts are in Art/prompts.txt.
Technical scripts crop/register/pack those images; they do not synthesize art.
The final images are installed in Assets/Characters/KingVenguard/V2/Spine/
King_Vanguard.png and AreaCompleted_Vanguard.png. Runtime changes are in that
folder's Scripts/KingVanguardFaceSkin.cs, KingVanguardMouthRig.cs, FaceRig,
FaceShapeModifier, CrownSkin and generated contour data.

The original skeleton binaries and animation clips remain intact. New meshes
retain source bone motion, but incompatible original facial FFD masks are not
blindly applied to new silhouettes. Native mouth and eyelid deformation remains
the animation driver. The approved Royal Signature IV outfit remains installed.

LIMITS / RECOVERED SOURCE TRACKS

The raw recovered area-king_area 1 and area-king_area_2 tracks still show extra
arm/sleeve variants when played without their original scene setup. portrait-_3
is a source layout/transform track with unsuitable standalone framing. These
tracks are not routed by the production character controller and were not
converted into new game activities in this facial repair. The production area
idle/win/clap routes were included in inspection. Do not interpret mathematical
checks across all 42 clips as artistic approval of those source-only tracks.

Android/device rendering and performance were not tested in this pass.
