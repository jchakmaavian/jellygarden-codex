# Color Ball V5.1

Refines the installed V5 prefab variant, without duplicating the booster or changing its gameplay sequence.

- Charged motion now has continuous bounded local tremble, shared body/surface tilt, curved band bunching and moving highlights. Reveal, idle and the spin/shutter clock remain intact.
- A layered white/pale-gold aura surrounds the bands. The beam origin follows the animated pivot after its final pose is drawn; targeting still uses the logical cell anchor.
- Beam geometry and overlap timing are unchanged. Additive warm falloff, tapered ends and travelling energy strengthen the discharge. Contact remains compact and uses the existing conversion events.
- The existing 0.46-second ending now concentrates into white/gold light and an expanding luminous ring. Rocket scheduling is unchanged.
- Existing original audio clips are reused with quieter sustained charge, no orbit/motor loop in V5.1, shorter contact tails, clearer beam/final accents and local voice limits.
