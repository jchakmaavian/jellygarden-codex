# Royal Throne texture import audit (Android)

**Step 1 done (2026-09-26, iteration 40, decided by the user):** 166 of the 184 uncompressed scene textures are drawn by nothing
visible on 30 screens (home and its panels, the shops, How-to-play, levels 1/12/26/51/76/100, pause, win and fail), with the
hidden legacy map proven covered on home by a frame comparison. They now carry an Android override, ASTC 4x4 at normal quality and
their own max size: about 74.6 MB -> 22.3 MB (counting ASTC's whole 4x4 blocks; iteration 40 first gave the 8-bpp estimate, 20.4 MB). Excluded: `CompleteLabel`'s and `NoMoreMatches`' textures (still shown in play), the
9 visible ones, the 6 protected ones, and `gold.png` (NPOT with mipmaps, which Unity would not compress). The list and the
evidence are in `RALPH_STATE.md`, iteration 40.

**Steps 2 and 3 done (2026-09-26, iteration 41, decided by the user):** the 11 visible textures that are not protected art (the
seven home dialog icons, the win and shuffle banners, the tutorial finger, the fail cross) and the protected art (the logo, the
title crown, both castles, the wheel), plus `fb_logout-button`, which nothing visible draws: 17 textures with the same Android
override, about 31.1 MB -> 8.5 MB. Each was compared as a phone decodes it (compressed to ASTC 4x4 in memory and decoded back;
ASTC decoding is specified bit-exactly, so a conformant phone decodes these same texels, iteration 50):
PSNR 36.9-48.9 dB on the visible art and 42.7-61.9 dB on the protected art, with no difference visible at display size. 183 of
the 184 now use ASTC on Android, about 105.8 MB -> 30.9 MB counting whole blocks (the 8-bpp estimate below, 28.9 MB, ignores
them); only `gold.png` (0.11 MB) is left. The evidence is in `RALPH_STATE.md`, iteration 41.

Generated by the QA loop on 2026-09-25 from each texture's `.meta` (effective Android format and max size) and the image headers. Figures are GPU-memory estimates for the Android build (project default compression: ETC), not measurements. Nothing was changed: every compression change alters pixels, and part of this art is protected (logo, splash, home).

Scene textures stay in memory for as long as the scene is loaded, whether or not they are on screen. The hidden legacy map and the legacy menu banners count too.

## Largest saving: 184 uncompressed scene textures, 105.7 MB (the 77 that are 256 px or larger hold 99.0 MB)

Compressed at 8 bpp (ASTC 4x4 or ETC2 RGBA8) they would take about 28.9 MB, a saving of roughly 77 MB of resident memory. Recommended order:

1. **Legacy art that Royal Throne no longer shows** (map backgrounds, old menu banners hidden by `RoyalMenuSkin`): compressing it costs nothing visually. Confirm each asset is hidden first.
2. **Visible legacy and Royal UI art:** compress with an Android override (ASTC 4x4 keeps edges and gradients best) and compare on a device.
3. **Protected art (logo, title crown, castle, home castle):** only with the owner's approval and a side-by-side check.
4. **Project default:** consider ASTC instead of ETC for Android (Player Settings > Texture compression formats). Almost every device from 2016 on supports it, and it helps the 765 textures that are on Automatic.
