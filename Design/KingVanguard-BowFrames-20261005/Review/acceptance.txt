Replacement bow acceptance criteria
- Head-angle changes expose the top of the cap and crown; no vertical scaling of face or beard.
- Eye/nose/moustache occlusion changes in the direction of the approved downward reference.
- Single opaque animation frame at a time; no whole-portrait image dissolve.
- The character keeps the same identity, outfit and frame registration.
- Input and authored activity can reverse the bend from its current progress.
- Pose time freezes when paused, resets with the board, and never consumes input.
- Validate idle-to-bow, reverse at mid-bow, full return, and interruption by a major reaction.
- Preserve the prior version for rollback and do not deploy unreviewed generated art.
