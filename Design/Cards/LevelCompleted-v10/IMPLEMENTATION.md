# Individual Well Done! letters

The completion title now uses nine independently animated glyphs instead of deforming a whole-word image. Seven transparent PNGs were generated with the built-in imagegen tool from the approved cream/navy/gold reference style: W, e, l, D, o, n and !. Repeated e and l reuse their assets. Each letter has its own complete bevel and extrusion, so its sides remain finished during the reveal.

Final assets: Assets/JellyGarden/Resources/RoyalGameplay/Win/Letters/. selected-assets.json records the exact final prompts and generation source paths. generation-prompts.json records the initial set; the initial glowing o was rejected and replaced by the clean o listed in selected-assets.json. Source pixels and alpha are preserved byte-for-byte; asset-validation.json records source dimensions, measured alpha bounds and SHA256 hashes. glyphs.json supplies normalized UV crops without modifying the PNGs. Unity imports uncompressed RGBA32 at a 1024-pixel maximum dimension, no mipmaps, bilinear filtering and clamp wrapping.

RoyalWinTitleLetters constructs the nine letter images once. The first letter begins at 0.10 s after the card's star starts. Logical characters are staggered by 0.04 s, including the space before D. Each letter grows from 0.28x to 1.64x over 0.10 s, rebounds to 0.94x over 0.20 s, and settles to 1x over 0.10 s. Slight alternating rotation settles to zero. All letters are settled by 0.86 s. The lower shared baseline and limited peak keep the title clear of the crest. Motion off displays the completed word with the existing short opacity fade. Reopening resets the same hierarchy.

Verification: Unity 6000.5.10f1 compiled successfully. verify_title_letters.cs passed 112 checks with zero failures at 1080x1920, 720x1280 and 1536x2048, including progressive visibility, onset order, growth/rebound, fully opaque rendered meshes, final transforms, crown clearance, repeated letters, reduced motion and hierarchy reuse. Continue and close routed exactly once. Normal save keys, currency/lives/level and the Home catalog were restored; no runtime errors were recorded. Unity was left in clean Edit Mode in main.unity.

Runtime scope: new RoyalWinTitleLetters plus the seven assets/crop metadata, RoyalCards builder and RoyalWinCard title timing. The previous whole-title art remains available in the repository. No reward/economy, star, pillow, shared button or background behavior was changed.
