Re-render all 120 idle frames (Windows PowerShell 5.1, no other dependencies):

  1. Scale the fuse-less barrel to 512x512:
       ffmpeg -i generated\gpt25_tnt_without_fuse.png -vf scale=512:512:flags=lanczos nofuse_512.png

  2. Render:
       powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File render_idle.ps1 ^
         -Cs TntRig.cs ^
         -Src <path to bomb_barrel.png, the original TNT art> ^
         -Patch nofuse_512.png ^
         -Out <output folder> -From 0 -To 119

  3. Check (base row, drift, badge flicker, spark path/popping, loop seam):
       powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File check_idle.ps1 ^
         -Rig TntRig.cs -Chk IdleCheck.cs -Dir <output folder>

Idle motion is keyed in TntRig.cs as (frame, value) pairs over frames 0..119, evaluated with
wrap-around (CycEval), so any edit stays seamless:
  I_Scale, I_SqY      body breath
  I_HeadSY            head/rim delayed bob
  I_Bend              fuse bend at the tip (degrees)
  I_SparkScale        spark size envelope
  I_SparkBright       spark brightness envelope (above 1 whitens toward white-hot)
  I_Glow              warm glow halo
  SparkVariants       the six fixed spark shapes
  IdleSparkles        the three tiny sparkles near the fuse
Keep frame 0 at rest values if the reveal -> idle handoff must stay exact.
