Re-render all 120 frames (Windows PowerShell 5.1, no other dependencies):

  powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File render.ps1 ^
    -Cs TntRig.cs ^
    -Src <path to bomb_barrel.png, the original TNT art> ^
    -Patch <generated\gpt25_tnt_without_fuse.png scaled to 512x512> ^
    -Flames generated\gpt25_flame_tongues_3x3.png ^
    -Out <output folder> -From 0 -To 119 -Kc 0.84

Scale the fuse-less image to 512x512 first, e.g.:
  ffmpeg -i generated\gpt25_tnt_without_fuse.png -vf scale=512:512:flags=lanczos nofuse_512.png

Motion is keyed in TntRig.cs (K_Scale, K_SqX, K_SqY, K_Lift, K_Tilt, K_FuseExt, K_Sway,
K_Glow, K_Flame) as (frame, value) pairs over frames 0..119.
check.ps1 reports frame-to-frame change, drift and the idle-frame match.
