# Royal Throne app icon

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 14 September 2026 and installed
in `Assets/RoyalBranding`. PNG is used here for Unity's launcher-icon pipeline.
The existing approved in-game logo is unchanged.

The full icon is `royal-throne-icon.png`. Adaptive Android icons use the transparent
`royal-throne-icon-foreground.png` on a plain royal-blue background. The foreground
was resized with transparent padding to fit the central 640 pixels of a 1024 square.
Unity's default icon and all 18 Android Legacy/Round/Adaptive slots are assigned and
checked by `RoyalAppIconBuilder` (Shift+F6 outside Play Mode).

## Generation prompts

Full icon: “Create a finished square mobile game app icon for Royal Throne, a polished
royal match-3 game for all ages. Close-up single majestic gold crown with one large
faceted ruby centerpiece, supported by a rich blue shield/throne silhouette, on a
luminous deep royal-blue background. Crown dominates the composition with a strong
instantly recognizable silhouette, broad clean gold bevels, sculptural 3D game
rendering, warm highlights, restrained small star glints. Visually rich but simple
enough to read at 48px. Center the important silhouette within the middle 70% for
round/squircle Android masks; fill the entire square background edge to edge.
Palette: royal blue, gold, ruby red. No letters or words, no characters, no logo text,
no border frame, no watermark. Original icon, not a copy of Royal Match.”

Adaptive edit: “Produce the Android adaptive-icon foreground only on a genuinely
transparent square background. Preserve the exact gold/ruby crown and royal blue
throne/shield object, material rendering and colors. Remove all surrounding blue
background glow, rays and stray floating glints outside the main object. Scale the
entire intact crown/throne object down to fit within the central 62% of the square,
with wide equal fully transparent margins on all sides. Do not crop any part of the
crown or shield. No new symbols, no text, no rounded rectangle background.”
