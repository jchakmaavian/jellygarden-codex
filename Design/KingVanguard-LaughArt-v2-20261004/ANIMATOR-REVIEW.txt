King Vanguard - hearty laughter art revision

Status: visual development and replacement artwork, not a tested runtime rig.
Keep the approved Royal Signature IV costume and fitted crown.

Observed in the existing 512px Unity laugh frame (00045.png):
1. Mouth opening is too small to sell a loud belly laugh.
2. Eyes look like a held blink; the cheek lift does not support their closure.
3. Moustache obscures most of the upper lip and the mouth corners.
4. Beard remains close to its resting silhouette when the mouth opens.
5. Overall pose reads as a gentle chuckle rather than three forceful Ho beats.

Confirmed in source:
KingVanguardFaceSkin hides the native independent cheeks and uses baked
cheeks plus a continuous beard. KingVanguardLaughter samples native blink
timelines and applies small whole-body motion (0.65 degrees sway, 1.2 percent
squash). The mouth opens inside this largely unchanged facial silhouette.
These are presentation limitations; the earlier tooth containment checks
passing does not prove the laugh is expressive or visually satisfactory.

New construction:
The generated target, King-Loud-Laugh-Target.png, establishes the expression.
It shows an upper lip beneath lifted moustache lobes, a connected upper tooth
row, shaded cavity, low tongue, cheek compression and lowered beard. Its
head tilt is a pose reference, not a suitable bind pose: replacement pieces
must be painted level and front-on. Avoid copying the tilt into each sprite.
- Skull group: crown, hair, ears, nose, brows, upper teeth.
- Cheek group: warm cheek lift plus the matching lower eyelid compression.
- Moustache: raised corners and a clear central opening above the upper lip.
- Mouth module: visible upper lip, shaded cavity, one anchored upper tooth
  row, low tongue and lower lip. Keep these coherent through every pose.
- Jaw group: lower lip, chin transition and lower beard move together.
- Crown remains rigid relative to skull; never bounce it independently.
- Drive jaw, cheeks and shoulder impulses from the same three syllable
  events. Use delayed, smaller beard follow-through rather than detached
  mouth movement. Blend in/out; restore normal tracking after the laugh.

Acceptance checks required before replacement in the main game:
- Mouth silhouette readable in the small HUD, not only a large portrait.
- Upper teeth attached to upper gum; lower lip follows jaw without sliding.
- No double teeth, skin seams, eye gaps, white fringe or opaque background.
- Moustache does not cover the upper lip throughout the three Ho peaks.
- Crown stays fitted across lean, bounce and recovery.
- Test transitions, interruption, pause and recovery at 15/30/60 fps.
- Validate alpha, attachment pivots and draw order after actual atlas import.

The old laughter staging package is superseded as a visual target. Do not
install it merely because its numerical containment tests passed.

Generated asset review:
Mouth-Peak is a single coherent painted mouth pose. Teeth cannot slide as
separately transformed sprites in this pose. It is not a complete viseme
set: a narrow Ho pose and a recovery pose are still needed for animation.
Use mesh rows or separate poses to keep tooth height constant as the jaw
moves. Uniformly scaling the entire mouth would squash the teeth again.
The generated art has near-opaque interiors (alpha approximately 252-253)
and very faint edge pixels. Inspect edges after atlas packing; the PNG
alpha check alone does not establish clean appearance in game.

Delivery verification:
Four source PNGs decode successfully as RGBA, have genuinely transparent
corners, and retain padding around their alpha>=8 silhouettes. See
art-validation.json and rerun validate_art.py to reproduce these checks.
An image-generation edge refinement was applied to mouth and eyes. It
reduced but did not eliminate all saturated edge candidates. Final source
inspection still found 688 yellow candidates on the eye image and 160 on
the mouth (alpha>=128); mouth red pixels may include legitimate shading.
Do not describe this as a finished clean-edge atlas or production-ready rig.
The paired eyelids need atlas separation. Jaw/beard and lips need fitting
against a clean face underlay. Runtime movement and HUD readability have
not been tested for these new assets. No main Assets files were changed.
