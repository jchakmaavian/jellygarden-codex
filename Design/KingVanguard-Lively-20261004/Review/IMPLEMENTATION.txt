King Vanguard lively portrait refinement - 2026-10-04

The approved King artwork and silent audio behavior are preserved. This revision changes live portrait animation, expression correspondence and motion response. No generated source PNG was edited.

New acting: three spaced idle performances (curious glance, pleased nod, greeting wink); irregular blink timing with occasional double blink; warm touch smile; one nod when a visible hint is acquired; alternating approval nods; happy bounces; excited/extra-move anticipation and repeated lift; relief exhale; gentler worry and disappointed slump. Eye movement leads the head and shoulders. Fresh input overrides idle glances. The existing gameplay state machine still owns match/impact priority, cooldown, failure and low-move context.

Look-around activity: while idle without fresh player input or a visible hint, the King looks down to the playing area, scans left and right, returns through center, then visits all four portrait-frame corners. Each target eases over 0.52 seconds; the eyes lead the slower head and shoulders. A neutral rest separates cycles. Fresh mouse/touch input, hints and authored gameplay reactions interrupt the route smoothly and restart its rest interval. The activity observes gameplay without consuming moves or input.

Continuity repairs:
- Continuous Skeleton.Time replaces per-track clock differencing, so clip boundaries and looping worry do not restart motion.
- Critically damped motion channels preserve position and velocity across activity changes. Zero-time callbacks and pause do not change the visible pose.
- One normalized expression blend avoids stacking several fully opaque faces.
- A 168-vertex facial correspondence mesh aligns brow/eye/nose/moustache/lip/chin landmarks across six existing paintings. Each texture has independent UVs on the same moving geometry. This removes the obvious doubled feature positions of a plain dissolve.
- Mouth interiors remain opaque while their geometry closes, instead of dissolving skin over teeth. Crown and silhouette support points remain fixed relative to the head.
- Body/shoulder follow-through is slower than the head. Blinking and idle gestures use a continuous actor clock rather than clip progress.
