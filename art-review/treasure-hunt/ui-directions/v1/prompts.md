## 1 — Royal Storybook

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a polished high-fidelity design presentation for the portrait mobile match-3 adventure "King Leo and the Great Royal Treasure Hunt", for children and families.
Input image 1 is the actual Castle Gates room ART REFERENCE, not an edit target. Use this same sunny cream-stone castle with blue roofs, oak double door and smiling gold sun as the setting in the mockups. Input image 2 is Pip CHARACTER REFERENCE: a cheerful cream-and-blue folded-paper map dragon. Preserve his identity whenever shown.
Deliver ONE wide landscape image, about 2:1 aspect ratio, high resolution with exceptionally readable text. Layout: a clean presentation board with a modest concept title at top, then exactly FOUR large equal-size portrait screen mockups side by side in a SINGLE ROW, straight-on, entirely visible including bottoms. Slim rounded screen boundaries, no hardware devices, no perspective, no hand holding a phone. Each screen has roughly 393:852 proportions. Small labels above them, outside the screen: "HOME", "AREA TASKS", "BEFORE LEVEL", "LEVEL COMPLETE". These must be true detailed UI mockups, not a mood board or concept landscape. Fill the canvas generously with the four screens; keep outer margins modest.
Common screen content across all directions:
HOME: full-height castle art visible behind minimal controls; small top coin counter "5", gold-star counter "0", settings icon. Clear title "Castle Gates" and "2/4 complete". A small Pip map button. Near bottom one next-task card with a blue-and-gold flag icon, "Decorate with royal flags", star cost "1". One prominent button "Play Level 3". A compact bottom bar with illustrated icons and labels "Home", "Map", "Collection". The castle doorway and sun must remain mostly visible.
AREA TASKS: same room behind an elegant bottom sheet occupying about the lower 55% of the screen. Heading "Castle Gates", progress "2/4 complete", close button. Compact checked line "2 tasks complete". Main next-task row: blue-and-gold flag thumbnail, "Decorate with royal flags", cost one star. Smaller future row: lantern thumbnail, "Light the lanterns", cost one star. With zero stars, the one prominent action is "Play Level 3", not an enabled repair purchase. Show enough room above the sheet to appreciate the castle.
BEFORE LEVEL: attractive compact pre-level modal over a softly dimmed/blurred match-3 board with colourful gems. "Level 3", "Goal", "500 points". Three familiar booster icons in separate slots: rocket, bomb, rainbow ball, with a small "Boosters" label. One large "Play" button and a small close button. Strong whitespace and hierarchy, no paragraphs or character dialogue.
LEVEL COMPLETE: celebratory modal over a softly dimmed/blurred gem board, "Level Complete!", "Level 3", one big GOLD STAR reward, "+1 Star", and one large "Back to Castle" button. Small Pip reaction permitted near the ribbon but star and button stay dominant. No three-star rating, no keys awarded, no extra currency rewards.
Readable friendly rounded type with dark text on light panels. Main buttons large with white text, consistent shape across all four screens. All icons, buttons, sheets, ribbons, corners and shadows must use ONE coherent finish within this concept. No watermarks, tiny lorem ipsum, arbitrary text, hearts/lives, timers, ads, shops, red error badges or dialogue cards. Bright charming polished game art. Decorative UI must leave clear breathing room.

DIRECTION 1: ROYAL STORYBOOK.
Board title exactly "1. Royal Storybook"; subtitle "Warm ivory · Royal blue · Soft gold".
Premium illustrated storybook material language: warm smooth ivory paper panels, restrained softly rounded gold edging, deep royal-blue FABRIC ribbons, tiny folded-paper bookmark details inspired by Pip. Bright cream/blue/gold palette with a little mint for completed checks. Large clear royal-blue buttons with a gentle bevel, never neon green. Room artwork is the hero. Panels have softly arched tops and tasteful page-corner curls, not heavy ornate frames. Bottom navigation is a floating ivory tab strip with softly sculpted blue/gold icons. The pre-level card resembles a simple royal invitation; victory has a modest blue ribbon and radiant gold star. Background of the presentation board is very pale warm ivory. Confident, uncluttered, elegant, tactile, family-friendly. This is the calm storybook option; use generous whitespace and avoid dense embellishment.

DIRECTION 2: PIP'S TREASURE MAP.
Board title exactly "2. Pip's Treasure Map"; subtitle "Folded paper · Turquoise · Golden trails".
Make a distinct folded-map adventure interface, not a recoloured royal ribbon interface. Fresh pale ivory map paper, turquoise/teal action buttons, sky-blue compass/cartography details and small golden wax-seal fasteners. Large paper shapes have visible angular folded corners, overlapping map tabs, crisp light creases and cut-paper edges. Fine dotted route paths and a tiny compass motif appear in margins, never behind text. Use a small six-empty-key-slots foldout marked "0/6 keys" on the home map control, without hiding the room. The next-task card feels like a pinned map note. The task sheet unfolds like one large map section; progress is four route stops with two filled. Pre-level is a folded expedition card secured by one small round gold seal. Victory uses a paper rosette and gold star with turquoise paper strips, not a crown banner. Bottom navigation uses three map tabs. Pip peeks from one folded corner. Buttons are softly rounded cut-paper turquoise tabs with white text. Cream and mint presentation background, clean and bright, no dirty/brown aged parchment, no pirate motifs. Distinctive, playful adventure character with practical readable layouts.

Most energetic of the three, polished and cheerful, controlled decoration and generous readable space, no circus tents and no excessive glitter.
